## What you'll learn

Every concept is introduced by **breaking** the system first — so you understand *why* each piece exists, not just what it's called.

- **Scaling**: vertical (bigger machine) vs horizontal (more machines) and when to pick each
- **Load balancers**: what happens when requests need to be distributed — and the single point of failure you just created
- **Stateless servers**: why your login breaks across multiple servers and how to fix it with shared sessions (Redis)
- **Connection pools**: the hidden limit that crashes your database during traffic spikes
- **Read replicas**: offload reads, but accept that your app will be *a little wrong* on purpose
- **Caching**: trade correctness for speed — follower counts yes, bank balances never
- **Queues & workers**: stop making users wait for emails and notifications
- **Sharding**: the most powerful tool in the video — and the last one you should use

## Who it's for

Developers who can build and deploy an app but want to understand what happens when it **succeeds** — when traffic spikes, databases hit their limits, and one server is no longer enough. Also great for anyone preparing for system design interviews.
